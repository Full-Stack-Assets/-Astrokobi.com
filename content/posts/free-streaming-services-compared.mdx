Free ad-supported streaming services have become increasingly popular as alternatives to traditional cable subscriptions and paid streaming tiers. A recent survey of over 1,000 cord cutters revealed a clear hierarchy among the top options, with The Roku Channel, Pluto TV, and Tubi emerging as the most popular choices. The survey highlights the importance of device compatibility, content selection, and user interface quality in determining preferences. 
   <Callout type="takeaway">The Roku Channel received the highest level of support at 31.1 percent of respondents who named it the best choice.</Callout>
   ## What happened
   The Roku Channel launched in September 2017 and has since expanded to include dedicated sections for kids and family programming. Pluto TV, founded in 2013, distinguished itself through a linear channel format that mimics the channel-surfing experience of traditional broadcast and cable television. Tubi, which entered the market in April 2014, has maintained a strong focus on on-demand viewing with minimal barriers to entry.
   The concentration of reader preferences around these three services reflects several market dynamics, including the scale needed to secure diverse content deals and invest in reliable apps and websites. Major technology and media companies behind The Roku Channel, Pluto TV, and Tubi possess the resources to deliver consistent advertising experiences.
   The survey results also underscore the rapid expansion of free ad-supported streaming television services in recent years, which deliver extensive libraries of movies, television series, and other programming supported entirely by advertisements.
   ## Why it matters
