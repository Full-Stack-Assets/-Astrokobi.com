Swatch has launched a limited edition Moonswatch to commemorate the 1969 Apollo 11 moon landing. The new Mission To The Moon 1969 Moonswatch is the most limited of the Moonswatches, with only 1,969 pieces made in total. This special execution pays homage to Apollo 11's Moon landing in 1969.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Swatch Mission To The Moon 1969 Moonswatch is a limited edition watch that commemorates the Apollo 11 moon landing.</Callout>

## What happened
The Swatch Mission To The Moon 1969 Moonswatch is a limited edition watch that features eleven grams of gold, priced as if it were back in 1969. This new release comes after a period of anticipation, following the news cycle around this year's Swatch x Audemars Piguet launch.

The limited edition Moonswatch is part of Swatch's popular high/low collaboration. Given its limited production run, this Moonswatch is expected to be highly sought after by collectors.

## Why it matters
The Swatch Mission To The Moon 1969 Moonswatch matters because it is a unique tribute to a historic event in space exploration. The Apollo 11 moon landing was a groundbreaking achievement, and this limited edition watch serves as a reminder of its significance.
