This eclectic mix signals a deliberate push toward broader cultural representation and fresh gameplay dynamics. Fans will see the “Time Trip” theme in action as past, present, and future concepts collide throughout the season.

## What happened
CBS released the official bios and photos for the fourteen houseguests, highlighting the variety of professional backgrounds. The season’s theme, “Time Trip,” promises challenges that blend historical references with futuristic twists, according to the network. The series premiered on Thursday at 8 p.m. ET/PT, and live feeds will be available the next day on Paramount+, Pluto and YouTube for a limited window.
