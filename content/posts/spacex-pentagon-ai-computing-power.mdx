## What happened
The discussions between SpaceX and the Pentagon are part of a broader effort by the military to leverage AI to improve its operations. The Pentagon has been exploring ways to integrate AI into its systems, and providing computing power is a critical component of this effort. SpaceX, with its expertise in advanced technology and computing, is well-positioned to support the Pentagon's AI initiatives.

The Pentagon's AI push is driven by the need to improve its decision-making processes and operational efficiency. However, developing and deploying AI capabilities requires significant computing power, which is where SpaceX comes in.
