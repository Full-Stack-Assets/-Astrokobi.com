NASA has just awarded a major facilities‑support contract to keep its historic Ames Research Center humming in Silicon Valley. The award goes to Chugach Intelligence Solutions LLC, which will handle operations, maintenance, and repairs across the campus. With a potential value of about $158 million, the five‑year contract is designed to keep the center’s unique laboratories and testbeds ready for current and future missions. Ensuring reliable infrastructure is critical for the agency’s research partners and the myriad space‑flight projects that rely on Ames.
<Callout type="takeaway">A $158 million contract will secure Ames’s facilities for the next five years.</Callout>
## What happened
NASA selected Chugach Intelligence Solutions LLC to provide comprehensive operations, maintenance, and repair services for the Ames Research Center. The Facilities Support Services II contract covers the historic and specialized facilities that support NASA missions and tenant partners in the NASA Research Park.
The hybrid contract spans a five‑year period of performance, beginning on Aug. 13, with a 12‑month base period, four optional 12‑month extensions, and a possible six‑month extension. It combines cost‑plus‑award‑fee core requirements, a firm‑fixed‑price phase‑in, and indefinite‑delivery/indefinite‑quantity task orders to address routine and emergent needs.
The award’s maximum potential value is approximately $158 million, inclusive of all options and the IDIQ ceiling.
## Why it matters
Ames Research Center is a hub for aeronautics, astrobiology, and advanced technology development; its facilities must remain functional to support experiments, flight simulations, and collaborations with industry and academia. Reliable infrastructure reduces downtime, protects costly equipment, and ensures that mission‑critical research can proceed on schedule, directly influencing NASA’s ability to meet exploration goals.
