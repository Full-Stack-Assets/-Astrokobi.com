NASA's Hubble Space Telescope has captured a breathtaking image of a crimson cloud and stars in the stellar nursery LH 95, located in the Large Magellanic Cloud, a dwarf galaxy that orbits the Milky Way. This region is home to both low-mass infant stars and massive blue giant stars, known as a stellar association. The image showcases the brilliant blue and white stars shining against a crimson background of glowing gas. This stellar nursery is one of many in the Large Magellanic Cloud.

## What happened
The Hubble Space Telescope captured an image of the stellar nursery LH 95 on July 3, 2026. LH 95 is a region in the Large Magellanic Cloud, a dwarf galaxy that orbits the Milky Way. The image reveals a crimson background of glowing gas with brilliant blue and white stars.

The stellar nursery LH 95 is home to both low-mass infant stars and massive blue giant stars. These stars are part of what is known as a stellar association, one of many in the Large Magellanic Cloud.

## Why it matters

The Large Magellanic Cloud, being a dwarf galaxy, provides a unique environment for studying star formation under conditions different from those in the Milky Way.
