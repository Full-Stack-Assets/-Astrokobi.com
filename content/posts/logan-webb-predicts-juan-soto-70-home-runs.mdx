Logan Webb, the ace pitcher for the San Francisco Giants, recently shared his thoughts on New York Mets slugger Juan Soto, predicting that Soto could hit 70 home runs in the future. This statement came during MLB All-Star Media Day as Webb discussed the challenge of facing Soto. With a current batting average of .290, 21 home runs, 51 RBIs, a .405 on-base percentage, and a .562 slugging percentage, Soto has proven himself to be one of the most dangerous hitters in the league.

## What happened
Webb made the remark during MLB All-Star Media Day while discussing the challenge of facing Soto. The Mets outfielder enters the break batting .290 with 21 home runs, 51 RBIs, a .405 on-base percentage and a .562 slugging percentage after another standout first half. Webb added that Soto continues to improve every season and remains one of the toughest hitters in baseball to attack because of his elite strike-zone discipline.

Soto's production has helped anchor the Mets' lineup throughout the first half. Even after dealing with a minor calf issue earlier in the season, the four-time All-Star has remained one of the National League's most dangerous offensive players entering the break.

## Why it matters
Webb's praise also reflects the respect Soto has earned across Major League Baseball. Few pitchers have faced him without acknowledging his patience, power and ability to control an at-bat regardless of the count. With the All-Star Game set for Philadelphia, Soto will take the field carrying the confidence of both teammates and opponents.

## How to think about it
This prediction and praise from Webb underscore the high regard in which Soto is held by his peers. It also highlights Soto's potential to achieve rare milestones in baseball, such as hitting 70 home runs in a season.
