Spirit Electronics has announced a significant new managed-access offering, providing aerospace and defense customers with a secure pathway to advanced, U.S.-based semiconductor manufacturing. This initiative directly addresses the critical need for domestic wafer fabrication of custom ASICs and high-reliability integrated circuits, ensuring that vital mission needs are met with trusted, onshore production capacity. The move is poised to strengthen national defense capabilities and reduce reliance on foreign supply chains for sensitive technologies.

<Callout type="takeaway">
  Spirit Electronics is enabling secure, U.S.-based access to advanced 28nm CMOS semiconductor manufacturing for critical aerospace and defense programs.
</Callout>

## What happened
Spirit Electronics has launched a managed-access model that connects defense and aerospace program managers with mature, mixed-signal, advanced-node CMOS platforms manufactured entirely within the United States. This capability makes 28nm CMOS technology, long utilized in high-reliability commercial and industrial applications, now accessible to government Tier 1 programs. These programs often require stringent secure sourcing, long-term sustainment, and controlled supply-chain assurance.

The company's offering provides a structured interface for early program scoping, technical alignment, and lifecycle coordination, all operating under appropriate NDA and security frameworks. Spirit Electronics centralizes a range of vertically integrated services, including secure program oversight, supply-chain management, on-shore assembly and test, environmental screening, qualification, and lifecycle support. Their facility is DLA-suitable, AS9100, ISO9001, and CMMC Level 2 certified, ensuring a trusted environment for these critical components.

## Why it matters
This development is crucial for national security and the resilience of U.S. aerospace and defense programs. By providing a secure, domestic source for advanced semiconductors, it significantly mitigates risks associated with global supply chain vulnerabilities, geopolitical instability, and potential intellectual property theft. For program managers, it means faster deployment of mission-critical systems with assured component provenance and long-term sustainment capabilities.

Furthermore, this initiative supports the broader goal of strengthening the domestic microelectronics ecosystem. It fosters a more robust U.S. manufacturing base for high-reliability components, which is essential for maintaining technological superiority and strategic independence in an increasingly complex global landscape. The ability to source these components domestically reduces lead times and enhances the agility of defense programs.

## How to think about it
For aerospace and defense program managers, this managed-access offering represents a strategic imperative rather than just a procurement option. It's about de-risking future programs and ensuring that the foundational components of critical systems are impervious to external disruptions. Consider this a key enabler for maintaining operational readiness and technological advantage. The value lies not just in the chip itself, but in the assurance of its origin, security, and long-term availability within a controlled domestic ecosystem.

<Callout type="warning">
  While 28nm CMOS is an advanced and mature technology, it is important to note that this offering is tailored for high-reliability, secure applications within specific government programs, and does not represent access to the bleeding-edge nodes typically seen in consumer electronics.
</Callout>
